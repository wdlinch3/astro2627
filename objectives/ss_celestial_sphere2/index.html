Celestial Navigation — Part II

Use these objectives to guide your work on this packet and its related lessons. Some objectives recur across packets. Test scope is specified separately.

I am able to…

  • explain what diurnal motion is.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Diurnal motion / rotation / revolution
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.diurnal_motion.010

  • clearly distinguish rotation from revolution.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Diurnal motion / rotation / revolution
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.diurnal_motion.020

  • explain what the ecliptic and ecliptic plane are.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Ecliptic / equator / seasons
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.ecliptic_seasons.010

  • explain what the Earth’s equatorial plane is.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Ecliptic / equator / seasons
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.ecliptic_seasons.020

  • relate seasonal observations to the inclination of the Earth’s rotation axis.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Ecliptic / equator / seasons
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.ecliptic_seasons.030

  • explain what the equinoxes and solstices are, when they occur, and where the Earth is in its orbit when they occur.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Ecliptic / equator / seasons
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.ecliptic_seasons.050

  • relate the Sun’s changing declination and daily path to its rising and setting directions, noon altitude, and length of daylight at a specified observing latitude and season.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Ecliptic / equator / seasons
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.ecliptic_seasons.060

  • describe an analemma as the pattern of the Sun’s positions observed at the same clock time over a year.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Ecliptic / equator / seasons
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.ecliptic_seasons.070

  • locate specific objects in the sky using an appropriate coordinate system.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Celestial coordinates
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.coordinates.030

  • define right ascension and declination using the celestial equator, celestial poles, and vernal-equinox direction, and interpret their units and coordinate grids.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Celestial coordinates
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.coordinates.040

  • explain how horizontal coordinates depend on observing location and time, and how the equatorial grid moves relative to the horizon as Earth rotates.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Celestial coordinates
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.coordinates.050

  • derive and apply the relation between latitude, solar declination, and the Sun’s altitude at local solar noon, including equinox and solstice cases.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Celestial coordinates
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.coordinates.060

  • record an object’s altitude and azimuth with the observing location and date/time, and use a chart or sky map for the same observing conditions to support an identification.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Observation and instruments
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.observations.020

  • distinguish a constellation from an asterism and use recognizable star patterns and coordinate grids to locate objects, including Polaris and the neighborhood of the equatorial coordinate origin.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Star charts and constellations
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.star_charts.010

  • trace the Sun’s annual path through the zodiacal constellations on a star chart.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Star charts and constellations
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.star_charts.020

  • construct a planisphere appropriate to an observing latitude and use its date/time settings and horizon window to locate visible star patterns.

    Objective reference

    Celestial Sphere, Prominent Stars, Constellations · Star charts and constellations
    aa.ss.celestial_sphere.star_charts.030
