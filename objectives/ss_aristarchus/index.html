Advanced Astronomy · Learning objectives

Aristarchus

Use these objectives to guide your work on this packet and its related lessons. Some objectives recur across packets. Test scope is specified separately.

I am able to…

  • explain who Aristarchus was and what he believed about the Sun, planets, and stars.

    Objective reference

    Aristarchus of Samos · History
    aa.ss.aristarchus.history.010

  • explain some of the reasons Aristarchus’ model was rejected by his contemporaries, including arguments involving parallax, wind, and the motion of objects on Earth.

    Objective reference

    Aristarchus of Samos · History
    aa.ss.aristarchus.history.020

  • apply Aristarchus’ logic to modern observations to determine relative sizes of the Sun, Earth, and Moon.

    Objective reference

    Aristarchus of Samos · Sun–Earth–Moon
    aa.ss.aristarchus.sun_earth_moon.010

  • apply Aristarchus’ logic to modern observations to determine relative sizes of the Sun–Earth and Earth–Moon distances.

    Objective reference

    Aristarchus of Samos · Sun–Earth–Moon
    aa.ss.aristarchus.sun_earth_moon.020

  • apply Eratosthenes’ method to modern observations to determine the size of the Earth.

    Objective reference

    Aristarchus of Samos · Sun–Earth–Moon
    aa.ss.aristarchus.sun_earth_moon.030
