Astrodynamics (Part I)

Use these objectives to guide your work on this packet and its related lessons. Some objectives recur across packets. Test scope is specified separately.

I am able to…

  • apply Kepler’s Third Law to predict orbital periods or orbital radii from a table of known values.

    Objective reference

    Kepler’s Laws · Applications of Kepler’s Laws
    aa.ss.kepler.applications.040

  • generalize Kepler’s Third Law to other systems, such as the Galilean moons, and explain what changes and why.

    Objective reference

    Kepler’s Laws · Applications of Kepler’s Laws
    aa.ss.kepler.applications.050

  • explain what an inertial frame is and why it matters.

    Objective reference

    Astrodynamics · Newton’s First Law

  • use conservation of linear momentum in simple before-and-after situations.

    Objective reference

    Astrodynamics · Newton’s First Law
    aa.x.astrodynamics.n1.020

  • recognize that Newton’s Second Law holds only in inertial frames.

    Objective reference

    Astrodynamics · Newton’s Second Law

  • recognize and use Newton’s Second Law in multiple equivalent forms.

    Objective reference

    Astrodynamics · Newton’s Second Law

  • explain Newton’s Third Law and use it in simple problems.

    Objective reference

    Astrodynamics · Newton’s Third Law

  • identify Newton’s law of gravitation, explain its functional form, and determine the dimensions of Newton’s constant.

    Objective reference

    aa.x.astrodynamics.gravity.010

  • distinguish altitude above a body’s surface from distance to its center, calculate gravitational acceleration at either location, and explain orbiting astronauts’ apparent weightlessness as shared free fall.
