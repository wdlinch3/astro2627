Dimensional Analysis

Use these objectives to guide your work on this packet and its related lessons. Some objectives recur across packets. Test scope is specified separately.

I am able to…

  • understand the size of uncertainties and represent physical values accordingly.

    Objective reference

  • round to a reasonable number of figures when the uncertainty is not stated or is unknown.

  • distinguish dimensions such as length, mass, and time from units such as meters, kilograms, and seconds.

    Objective reference

    Dimensional Analysis · Dimensions vs. units
    aa.x.dimensional_analysis.units.010

  • convert between common units and choose reasonable units for very large or very small quantities.

    Objective reference

    Dimensional Analysis · Dimensions vs. units
    aa.x.dimensional_analysis.units.020

  • convert to and from SI units and interpret decimal exponents.

    Objective reference

    Dimensional Analysis · SI, dex, metric prefixes

  • interpret common metric prefixes across a wide range of scales.

    Objective reference

    Dimensional Analysis · SI, dex, metric prefixes
    aa.x.dimensional_analysis.prefixes.020

  • explain the meaning of fundamental dynamical quantities such as mass, distance, time, velocity, momentum, acceleration, force, energy, and power.

  • explain the distinction between mass and weight.
