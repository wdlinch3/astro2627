Astrodynamics (Part II)

Use these objectives to guide your work on this packet and its related lessons. Some objectives recur across packets. Test scope is specified separately.

I am able to…

  • calculate and compare kinetic, gravitational potential, and mechanical energy, including their dimensions, signs, and dependence on mass, speed, and separation.

    Objective reference

    Astrodynamics · Energy
    aa.x.astrodynamics.energy.010

  • use conservation of mechanical energy in gravitational problems, state the assumptions under which it applies, and explain why changing the zero of potential energy does not change physical predictions.

    Objective reference

    Astrodynamics · Energy
    aa.x.astrodynamics.energy.020

  • state the Virial Theorem, derive and use the kinetic–potential energy relation for a circular gravitational orbit, and distinguish this instantaneous relation from the orbit-averaged relation for a bound elliptical orbit.

    Objective reference

    Astrodynamics · Energy
    aa.x.astrodynamics.energy.030

  • interpret the slope of a potential-energy graph qualitatively to determine the direction and compare the strength of the associated force.

    Objective reference

    Astrodynamics · Energy
    aa.x.astrodynamics.energy.050

  • compare circular-orbit and escape speeds at the same distance, and use energy reasoning to determine whether escape is possible under specified assumptions.

    Objective reference

  • combine Newton’s laws to derive gravitational field strength, orbital speed, Newton’s version of Kepler’s Third Law, and escape speed.
